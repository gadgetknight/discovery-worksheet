How a pack gets researched

  1. Job postings for the office role — dispatcher, office manager, practice administrator. The real daily tasks and the real software, in the employer’s words.
  2. Vendor workflow pages — what the system of record actually does, and by omission what still lives in spreadsheets.
  3. Practitioner forums — the pain in the owner’s own words. Paraphrased, never invented, never attributed.
  4. Licensing boards and regulators — what’s required versus folklore.
  5. Completed worksheets — the best source; the reason pack ten will be better than pack one.

One session per pack. Thirty minutes of your review. Nothing un-reviewed reaches a customer.
