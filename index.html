Where the Time Goes

How this works
  • Answer in your own words. Specific beats polished. “Last Tuesday” beats “usually.”
  • Describe problems, not solutions. Figuring out the fix is the builder’s job. You only have to describe what actually happens.
  • Nothing is graded. A half-answer is more useful than a blank. Questions marked optional can be skipped if you’re short on time.
  • Your answers save in this browser as you type. Close the tab, come back later, pick up where you left off. (They stay on this device only.)
Three ground rules
  • One occasion, not a pattern. Wherever you can, tell me about a specific time it happened, with a day and a number.
  • In Parts 2–4, don’t use the words app, software, system, automate, dashboard, or AI. If you catch yourself writing “we need something that…”, stop and write down what happened instead.
  • Real beats tidy. The messy workaround you’re slightly embarrassed by is usually the most valuable thing on this page.
